**Scope.**

- Wire `node-graph-module-actions.js` (bind the page on open) and `node-graph-history.js` (re-render the page after undo).

- **The D-G1 prototype:** after the page works, build a short clickable prototype of "delete a module that a gradient references / duplicate a module" with the three options (drop params / copy into the new node / keep dangling) and give Argi the choice. Write his answer into plan section 4.1. Until then keep HP-03's skip-missing behavior.

**Rules.** No `title=`, no hover-only actions, 44 px rows, no timers (do not reuse `confirmNodeGraphDefaultButtonClick`), no edits to MasterBot's files; use `nodeGraphGlGraphNoteValueChrome(id)` after a position change so GPU sliders repaint.
