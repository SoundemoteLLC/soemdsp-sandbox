## Goal

A spectral effect that sounds hard-edged and blocky on purpose, with a display that shows exactly what you hear.

## Sound

- **No overlap:** rectangular windows and no crossfade between frames, so each frame starts and stops hard.
- **Bin snap:** each partial jumps to its bin's center frequency instead of gliding.
- **Frame hold:** sample-and-hold the frame for N hops before updating, for chunky time steps.
- **Level quantize:** bin magnitudes snap to a few loudness steps.

## Display

- One flat cell per bin per frame, with thin dark lines between bands and no interpolation or blur. What you see is exactly what you hear.
- Reference look (Argi's image): horizontal bands of flat rectangular cells, warm orange and red for loud, dark grey and green for quiet. The cells are chunky in time, and the edges are hard.
