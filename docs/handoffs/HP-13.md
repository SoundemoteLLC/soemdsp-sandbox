## HP-13: Velocity Mutator
**Owner:** Misty · **Size:** L · **Depends on:** HP-11, HP-12, Argi's go (plan: "only after the Pitch Mutator workflow is solid") · **Phase:** E

**D-M4 (decided t449u).** Argi: "yes, gate and trigger always take velocity into account, and velocity is a handy extra "last velocity" value". Gate/Trigger **height** always carries velocity (`docs/GATES_TRIGGERS.md`). The Velocity jack is an extra "last velocity" value, not the only source.

**Scope.** A second Mutator type on the shared engine: axis = velocity 0..1 from Gate/Trigger height, with the Velocity jack available as last-velocity, same overlay frame and tabs, "play a velocity, tweak, play another velocity, tweak". Optional later: a small embedded Sturmian rhythm player for audition (listed as a leftover idea in the Mutator plan section 13). PLAIN / STURM pad is an idea, not adopted; decide with Argi when starting. Same native table hook as HP-11; polyphonic per-voice velocity needs the Voice Velocity bus decided in HP-09/HP-11.

**Acceptance.** Cutoff or level follows velocity per voice in a poly patch; headless parity test native vs live; overlay shows velocity axis 0..1 with hairline-sharp UI; no tooltips, no timers.
